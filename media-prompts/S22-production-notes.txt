Technical caveats:
- €200, €600, €80/h, 40h, and 8h are hypothetical; not vendor prices or measured workloads.
- Combined allocated monthly costs €3,400 and €1,240; difference €2,160. Engineering capacity released 32h; additional cash infrastructure cost €400. Savings in allocated costs are not necessarily realized cash savings.
- Simplified comparison omits acquisition, migration, capability, resilience and other lifecycle costs.
- Opportunity cost is the forgone value of the next-best use of resources, not an additional line item added to the allocated labor cost.
- Managed databases retain customer responsibilities; S21 provides context.
- Open-source licensing varies; zero licence fee is an explicit hypothetical example.
- Provider-specific functionality may be justified despite switching costs.
- Four TCO categories are a teaching framework, not sequential software development phases.
